The synth stations run on a shared clock. Press play and you join the rotation wherever it is. Leave, come back, and it carried on without you.

How do I use the wheel?

Drag around the ring to scroll, press the center to choose. MENU goes back, ▶︎❚❚ plays and stops, and ⏮ ⏭ change station. On Now Playing the wheel is the volume. Arrow keys, Return, Escape and Space work too.

Is there a Mac app?

Yes. Download HIFI for Mac, open the file and drag HIFI to Applications. It’s signed and checked by Apple, so it opens like any other app.

Is it free?

Yes. There’s no HIFI account or subscription.

What does the heart do?

Connect Spotify (in Settings or Playlists) and the ♥ next to a live song saves it to your Liked Songs. Your playlists show up on the wheel, with Nate’s Software HIFI at the top.

Can I listen inside Nate’s Software?

Yes. Open HIFI on the desktop. It stays in its own window, and minimizing it keeps the music playing.

Where does the music come from?

Radio Paradise and SomaFM supply their own live broadcasts. The Synth Sounds are original instrumentals made for HIFI, plus a few owner-supplied AI-generated tracks; their artist names are made up.
