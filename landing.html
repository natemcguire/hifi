Radio rules apply

You tune in.
The music is already there.

Our original stations run on a shared clock. Press Play and you join the current point in the rotation. Leave, come back, and the station has carried on without you.

Radio Paradise and the SomaFM stations are live streams. Our other stations mix owner-supplied AI-generated tracks with original procedural instrumentals. The artist aliases in the seed catalog are fictional; HIFI Studio credits our own compositions.

Is it free?

Yes. There’s no HIFI account or subscription to listen.

Why doesn’t it start automatically?

Sound should be your decision. Press Play once to start the audio and unlock the visualizations.

Can I listen inside Nate’s Software?

Yes. Open HIFI on the desktop. It stays in its own app window, and minimizing it keeps the music playing.

Is the music generated live?

The original music is rendered ahead of time and played in a repeating rotation. The visualizations are generated live from the actual sound. Radio Paradise and SomaFM supply their own live broadcasts.
