Use your own Spotify app

Spotify lets HIFI's app sign in only a few listeners. Make your own free Spotify app and HIFI connects through it instead. It takes two minutes and needs Spotify Premium.

  1. Open Spotify's developer dashboard and press Create app.Open dashboard ↗
  2. Name it anything. Under Redirect URIs, add this address:https://nates-software.com/hifi/spotify/callback
  3. Tick Web API, agree to the terms and press Save.
  4. Copy the Client ID from your app's page and paste it here. Never the Client Secret.
