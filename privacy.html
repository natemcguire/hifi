Privacy

HIFI doesn’t collect
anything about you.

There’s no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, on the web or in the iPhone, iPad and Mac apps.

To play music, the app asks for the station list, the song now playing and the music files from hifi.nates-software.com. Live stations stream straight from Radio Paradise, SomaFM and Exclusive Radio. Like any website, those servers see your IP address when they send the audio. HIFI doesn’t store it or link it to you.

Your volume and last station are saved on your own device and never leave it.

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/natemcguire/hifi.
